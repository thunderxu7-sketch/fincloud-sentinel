# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.
Be respectful, give actionable technical feedback, protect confidential information, and avoid
claims that could mislead customers about financial or regulatory guarantees.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to `thunderxu7@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove comments,
commits, issues, or contributions that violate these expectations.
